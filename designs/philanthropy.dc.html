Why I give

Success is measured in service

I've learned that real success isn't found in titles. It's found in what we do with what we've been given: how we uplift, nurture and restore the people around us.

My giving grows from the same place as everything else I do: faith, family and a belief that healing starts from within. I want every woman to know she is worthy of love, capable of growth and never on her own.

— Leya
