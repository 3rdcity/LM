About me

Hi, I'm Leya. Living life in full colour

I'm Leya Mnangagwa, a woman devoted to living life in full colour, guided by faith, purpose and grace.

I've worn many hats: entrepreneur, founder, mentor, dreamer. But above all, I'm a wife and mother of four who believes that growth begins with truth and healing starts from within. My journey has taught me that real success is found not in titles but in service, in using what we have to uplift, nurture and restore others.
